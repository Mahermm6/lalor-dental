# About Lalor Dental Clinic

## Our Dentist — Dr. Eyad Almashaal
Dr. Eyad Almashaal is the principal dentist at Lalor Dental Clinic. He holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and is a member of the Australian Dental Association. With over 10 years of experience, Dr. Almashaal provides gentle, patient-centred dental care to families across Lalor and the northern suburbs of Melbourne.

## Our Philosophy
We believe great dental care starts with listening. Every patient is treated as an individual with unique needs and goals. Our clinic is welcoming, modern, and stress-free for patients of all ages.
